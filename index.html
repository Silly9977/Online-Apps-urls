Privacy Policy for LumiGroves Effective date: 19 September 2026 Last updated: 19 September 2026 LumiGroves ("the app") is a puzzle game developed by SillyDev ("we", "us"). This policy explains exactly what the app does with data. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past. The short version We do not have servers, and we do not have your data. Everything the app remembers — your progress, your streaks, your settings, the spirits you have found — is stored on your own device and never sent to us. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to. We show advertisements. Google AdMob serves these, and Google collects data to do it. That is the only third party involved, and it is described in full below. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Information stored on your device The app saves the following locally, using your device's own app storage: Puzzle progress and which groves you have completed Your Daily Challenge history and streak Hint and reveal balances, and your spirit collection Settings such as sound, haptics and reduced motion Whether you have completed the tutorial This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, we do not receive a copy of it, and we could not retrieve it if you asked us to. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android Settings. Information collected by advertising LumiGroves shows rewarded advertisements — short videos you choose to watch in exchange for hints, reveals or extra attempts — and an occasional video between levels that can always be skipped. We do not show banner ads, and we do not show ads that interrupt a puzzle in progress. These are served by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google may collect and process: Your device's Advertising ID (a resettable identifier) Your device's IP address, and from it an approximate, coarse location Device information such as model, operating system version and language Information about ads shown to you, and whether you interacted with them This processing is carried out by Google, not by us. We never receive this information; we receive only aggregate reporting about how many ads were shown. Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy Controlling advertising on your device You can limit or reset advertising identification at any time: Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID Resetting the ID prevents past ad activity being linked to future activity. Deleting it stops personalised advertising entirely. The app continues to work either way. Consent in the European Economic Area and the UK Where the law requires consent before personalised advertising or non-essential tracking, the app presents Google's consent form on first launch and your choice is respected for all subsequent ad requests. You can change your choice later from the app's Settings screen. If you decline personalised advertising, the app still works and you can still earn rewards by watching ads — the ads shown are simply non-personalised. What we do not collect We want to be specific about this, because many privacy policies describe practices their app does not actually have. No analytics. The app contains no analytics SDK. No usage events, screen views, session lengths or behavioural data are transmitted anywhere. No crash reporting. No crash or diagnostic data is sent to us or to any third party. No account, no profile, no email address, no phone number. No precise location. The app never requests location permission. No contacts, calendar, photos, microphone, camera or health data. The app requests none of these permissions and cannot access them. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond the AdMob processing described above. Permissions the app requests, and why Permission Why Internet Required to load advertisements. The puzzles themselves work fully offline. Notifications Only to send an optional daily reminder to play. Scheduled on your device; no notification is sent from a server. You can disable this in the app or in Android settings. Vibrate Haptic feedback when placing a piece. Can be turned off in Settings. Modify audio settings Required by the audio library used for sound effects. Run at startup Allows a scheduled daily reminder to survive a device restart. Storage Included by a system library used for temporary files. The app writes only to its own private storage, and does not read or write your photo library or any of your files. Sharing a result If you choose to share a result card, the image is created in the app's own private storage and handed to Android's share sheet. You choose where it goes. The app does not access your photo library and does not upload the image anywhere. Purchases LumiGroves may offer a one-time purchase to remove advertisements. If you make a purchase, it is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your payment card, billing address or any financial information. Google tells the app only whether the purchase is active. Google Play's handling of payment data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy linked above. Children LumiGroves is a general-audience game. It is not directed to children under 13, and within the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom it is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone and has no account system, there is no profile for us to hold or delete. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at the address below and we will act on it. Your rights Because we hold no personal data about you on any system we control, there is no file for us to retrieve, correct or erase. You can delete everything the app has stored by uninstalling it or clearing its data. For the data that Google processes for advertising, you can exercise your rights directly with Google using the links in the advertising section above, and through your device's advertising settings. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom (GDPR) You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Where we rely on consent — which for this app means advertising consent — you may withdraw it at any time without affecting anything done before you withdrew it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA) You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of its sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information. The advertising described above may constitute "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CPRA; you can opt out using your device's advertising settings or, in regions where it is presented, the in-app consent form. To make any request, contact us at the address below. Data retention On your device: kept until you uninstall the app or clear its data. With us: nothing. We operate no servers and store no user data. With Google: governed by Google's retention practices, described in the Google Privacy Policy. International transfers We do not transfer your data anywhere, because we do not receive any. Google operates globally and may process advertising data outside your country, including outside the EEA, using transfer mechanisms described in its own policy. Changes to this policy If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top and post the revised version at this address. If a change materially affects how data is handled, we will seek your consent where the law requires it.
