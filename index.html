LumiDoku – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

LumiDoku ("the game", "we") is a logic puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: we do not ask for, collect or sell your personal information. The game works without an account, and your progress stays on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress – levels, puzzles solved, streaks, badges, settings, and whether you bought "Remove Ads" – is saved only on your phone. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or using Settings → Reset All Game Data, deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows optional ads through Google AdMob – mostly rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and how you interact with ads. This is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Advertising choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. In-app purchase (Google Play)

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so it can turn ads off and restore your purchase if you reinstall.

4. Error reports

If something goes wrong, the game keeps a short error log on your device (the error message and the screens leading up to it – no personal data). It never leaves your phone automatically. You can choose to send it to us with Settings → Report a problem, which opens your phone's share menu so you decide where it goes. Google Play may also provide us with anonymous crash and performance statistics through its Android vitals service.

5. Analytics

The game does not currently use any third-party analytics service. If this changes, this policy will be updated first, and the game's Share anonymous data setting lets you turn it off.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game can send you gentle local reminders (for example, about the Daily Challenge). They are scheduled on your device; no data is sent to a server. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in Android settings.

7. Sharing

When you share a result or a challenge code, the game opens your phone's share menu. What you share, and with whom, is entirely your choice.

8. Children

LumiDoku is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, there is nothing about you stored on our side to access, correct or delete. For data collected by Google for advertising, please use the controls described in section 2 or contact Google.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
